* ✅ **Network effects:** Federated learning \= we improve faster than centralized approach  
* ✅ **Domain expertise:** We have materials scientists, they don't (harder to copy than pure software)

**When this happens:** Likely 2027-2028 (by then we should have platform lock-in)

### **Risk 3: Market Adoption Slower Than Expected**

**The risk:** Manufacturers are conservative, don't trust AI

**Likelihood:** Medium (industrial sales cycles are slow)

**Impact:** Medium (delays revenue, not fatal)

**Mitigation:**

* ✅ **Proof points:** Publish Nature paper (credibility with technical buyers)

**The risk:** CATL or Chinese competitors copy our designs without paying royalties

**Likelihood:** High (China's track record on IP)

**Impact:** Medium (limits royalty revenue in China)

**Mitigation:**

* ✅ **Geographic focus:** Target US/EU/Japan/India first (strong IP enforcement)  
* ✅ **Trade secrets:** Keep some key innovations as trade secrets (not patentable)  
* ✅ **Operational security:** Federated learning \= data never centralizes in one place  
* ✅ **Strategic alliances:** Partner with governments (IndiaAI, US DOE) for geopolitical support

**Acceptance:** We may never monetize China fully (but 70% of global EV production is outside China)

**Impact:** High (IP is in people's heads during early phase)

**Mitigation:**

* ✅ **Documentation:** Codify all scientific knowledge in papers, repos, wikis

* ✅ **Retention:** Competitive comp, meaningful equity, inspiring mission  
* ✅ **Succession plan:** Identify internal candidates for leadership roles

### **Risk 6: Model Bias or Hallucination**

**The risk:** AI confidently predicts wrong answer, customer builds it, it fails catastrophically

**Likelihood:** Medium (all ML models have failure modes)

**Impact:** High (liability, reputation damage, customer churn)

**Mitigation:**

* ✅ **Confidence scores:** Always output uncertainty estimates  
* ✅ **Human-in-loop:** Require expert review for mission-critical designs  
* ✅ **Validation protocols:** "AI generates, human validates, lab confirms"  
* ✅ **Insurance:** Carry errors & omissions insurance ($10M+ coverage)  
* ✅ **Transparency:** Show customers the training data distribution (when to trust vs not trust)

**Legal protection:** Terms of service clarify we provide "decision support" not "autonomous decisions"
