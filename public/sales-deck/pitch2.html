Core insight

Shodh removes the human bottleneck through inverse design

Shodh is designed to reason across hundreds of coupled physical variables.

It does not only ask:

“What happens if we try this?”

“What candidate, recipe, geometry, or process window should exist to hit this target?”

Proof 1

Exothermic reaction optimization

Problem

An exothermic reaction lost yield because hotspots and dead zones created secondary impurities.

Shodh found

A high-residence-time thermal zone near the feed / mixing region.

Shodh generated

New feed angle, feed rate, RPM curve, jacket temperature profile, and static insert geometry.

Message

Shodh does not only fit plant data. It finds the physical failure mode and generates a better operating policy.

Proof 2

Batch-to-continuous / reactor hardware

Problem

A high-value process had poor gas-liquid contact, long residence time, pressure-drop issues, and poor selectivity.

Shodh generated

A continuous-flow TPMS / gyroid-style reactor insert that increased interfacial area without creating packed-bed pressure penalties.

Message

Shodh invents process hardware, not just operating recipes.
