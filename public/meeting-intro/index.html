Why process knowledge matters

Discovery is only the beginning.

The hardest part of manufacturing is not discovering a product, but knowing exactly how to make it reliably at industrial scale.

This process knowledge is built through years of factory experience and is usually kept as closely guarded intellectual property.

Inverse design

Start with the outcome. Work backwards to the factory.

Shodh starts with the required outcome, such as a target yield, material property or production cost. The model then works backwards to recommend the product design, process conditions and factory settings needed to achieve it.
