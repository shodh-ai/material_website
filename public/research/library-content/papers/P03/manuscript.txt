ABSTRACT
An antibody representation should distinguish changes in complementarity-
determining region (CDR) geometry without confusing them with a rigid change
of pose. Coordinate reconstruction alone does not establish this distinction. We
examine a frozen coarse-grained antibody tokenizer with 32 carrier and 160
learned values per residue, using archived reconstruction comparisons, stored-
byte transformation audits and source-level residue-retention evidence. On the
same 103,680 historical frames, the reported median bead error is 0.0121 Å with
an exact decoder sidecar, 0.0510 Å with a token-derived header and 0.0726 Å
with direct carrier decoding. A separate 256-structure production-path audit has
median learned-lane rotation cosine 0.1782 despite a small decoded rotation dis-
crepancy of 0.02155 Å. A matched 1,000-structure counterfactual identiﬁes axis-
aligned reboxing and recanonicalization as a major ampliﬁer of pose sensitivity,
but not its sole source. We specify a joint heavy/light-chain framework align-
ment that preserves internal geometry analytically and deﬁne CDR-speciﬁc, anti-
collapse and external-comparison tests for its evaluation. The contribution is a
scoped empirical diagnosis and a testable antibody representation design; a suc-
cessful pose-robust repair, downstream beneﬁt and external superiority are not yet
demonstrated. Corpus accounting distinguishes 7.568 billion accepted bead-row
opportunities from a fully persisted token bank.
1 I NTRODUCTION
Antibody structures combine a comparatively conserved framework with variable loops that con-
tribute to antigen recognition. A representation that retains the framework but loses the geometry or
identity of a complementarity-determining region can be numerically accurate in an average sense
while being unsuitable for an antibody-speciﬁc task. Conversely, two coordinate ﬁles related only
by rotation and translation describe the same isolated structure. Their differences should not dom-
inate a representation intended for pose-independent retrieval or learning. These requirements are
complementary: nuisance-pose suppression is useful only if meaningful internal variation remains
accessible.
Antibody-speciﬁc structure prediction has made large computational structural collections practical.
IgFold and ImmuneBuilder illustrate the availability of predicted structures and the importance of
separately evaluating difﬁcult CDR regions ( Ruffolo et al. , 2023; Abanades et al. , 2023). Such
predictions supply structural hypotheses, not experimentally veriﬁed conformational ensembles. A
tokenizer that accurately reconstructs a predicted or simulated input has demonstrated ﬁdelity to that
input; it has not independently established its biological accuracy, antigen afﬁnity or developability.
This distinction becomes particularly important when a large number of conformations are generated
around each seed structure.
Our setting is the LUCAN/LURTEE Lobe 1-C antibody workstream, which uses one C α bead per
amino-acid residue. It is distinct from the protein–ligand Lobe 1-CG workstream, despite the his-
torical reuse of the internal symbol L1CG. The frozen antibody codec combines explicit geometric

manuscript, not a claim that a repaired tokenizer has already outperformed antibody representation
methods.
2 R ELATED WORK
Antibody structures, identities and local evaluation. SAbDab organizes antibody structures
with heavy/light-chain pairing and structural annotations ( Dunbar et al. , 2014). ANARCI as-
signs standardized antigen-receptor numbering, allowing equivalent residue positions to be matched
across variable-length sequences ( Dunbar and Deane , 2016). These resources motivate correspon-
dence based on chain identity and numbering rather than a ﬁxed sequence preﬁx. IgFold and Im-
muneBuilder predict antibody structures from sequence and evaluate loop-speciﬁc accuracy ( Ruf-
folo et al. , 2023; Abanades et al. , 2023). Our task is different: encoding and recovering supplied
Cα conformations. Their prediction scores cannot be compared directly with our reconstruction
errors, and a C α codec does not recover full backbone dihedrals or side-chain interactions without
an additional, separately validated reconstruction stage.
Antibody and protein structural tokenization. Igloo encodes antibody-loop sequence and
backbone-dihedral information and evaluates structural retrieval and incorporation into language
models ( Fang et al. , 2026). It is therefore a closer representation comparator than a crystal-string
tokenizer, but its loop-level, richer-input contract differs from a whole-antibody C α codec. S 2ALM
combines sequence and structure information using Foldseek-derived 3Di tokens and evaluates an-
tibody tasks ( Yin et al., 2025). Foldseek’s structural alphabet encodes local tertiary interactions for
efﬁcient structure search ( van Kempen et al., 2024). Neither a structural alphabet nor a task embed-
ding should be assumed to provide an invertible Cartesian decoder. Their evaluation requires shared
applicable endpoints rather than invented reconstruction capabilities.
 
Equivariance and canonical frames. EGNN constructs Euclidean-equivariant graph networks
(Satorras et al. , 2021). A message-passing network with invariant pairwise distances is not auto-
matically invariant if its node inputs also contain absolute fractional coordinates and oriented cell
components. Frame Averaging provides a systematic alternative for adapting networks to sym-
metries ( Puny et al. , 2022); work on equivariant frames also establishes limitations on globally
continuous canonicalization ( Dym et al. , 2024). Our proposed reference ﬁt is an antibody-speciﬁc,
correspondence-conditioned construction, not a new general equivariance theorem. It requires well-
conditioned anchors and must be evaluated after quantization.

The reviewed source speciﬁes approximately 3.90 million parameters in the frozen specialist.

static-conformation antibody branch. Box features contain nine oriented components, three row

ble 1). Replacing only the exact header with a header recovered from quantized carrier ﬁelds raises
median bead error from 0.012072 to 0.051002 Å. The result establishes sensitivity to the available
metadata, not a defect in using declared side information. Direct carrier reconstruction is less ac-
curate still. Conversely, these measurements do not show that the learned representation alone is
sufﬁcient, or that its information is biologically useful. A learned-block shufﬂe/zero experiment
with unchanged exact metadata is needed to quantify the residual contribution more directly.
The separate 2,000-antibody canary reports 32,000 frames and 7,408,848 persisted rows, with me-

6.4 R ESIDUE COMPLETENESS IS A SEPARATE FAILURE AXIS
The historical July handoff documents truncation of each chain at 115 residues and reports clipped
CDR-H3 segments and FR4 loss. It reports 15,116 CDR-H3-clipped antibodies (10.16%) and
92.10% FR4 clipping in its sequence audit, together with a limited longer-input probe. The un-
derlying per-antibody varlen_analysis.json was not available in the inspected evidence set;
these ﬁgures are historical reports, not newly veriﬁed prevalence estimates. The source preregis-
tration independently retains the Tier-1 CDR-H3/FR4 truncation issue as unresolved, and explicitly
states that excluding increment archives with N > 256 does not repair it ( Shodh AI Research ,
2026d;a).
The mechanism is straightforward. A preﬁx map T115(s) = s1:115 is non-injective: distinct full
chains sharing a preﬁx become identical inputs. Any encoder composed with that map necessarily
loses the distinguishing sufﬁx. Reconstruction against T115(s) cannot diagnose the omitted residues.
This observation motivates insertion-aware identity and full-residue admission; it is not a quantita-
tive demonstration that every structure in the accepted increment is truncated, nor that a completed
full-corpus repair exists.
6.5 S CALE ACCOUNTING DOES NOT ESTABLISH DIVERSITY OR PERSISTENCE
The reconciled corpus contains 2,042,821 antibody seed records and 32,685,136 frame records, cor-
responding to 7,568,346,192 accepted real-bead rows: 5,528,711,504 base rows plus 2,039,634,688
accepted increment rows. These are coordinate-cache-backed row opportunities, not a retained full
[N, 192] token bank. The physically persisted non-additive canary contains 7,408,848 rows. Full
tensor materialization at the current width would require 2,906,244,937,728 bytes before sidecars
and container overhead ( Shodh AI Research, 2026e).
The exposure audit reports an exact distinct-token-structure union for the increment, but not for
the combined base/increment corpus because the base global token-hash payload was not retained.
Zero overlap under a ﬁrst-115-residue production key is not full-sequence identity certiﬁcation.
Sixteen conformations from one antibody also do not provide sixteen independent folds. These
qualiﬁcations prevent scale, conformational sampling, unique biology and storage persistence from
being conﬂated.

7.2 A NALYTIC SCOPE AND FINITE -PRECISION RISKS
Under ﬁxed, valid biological correspondence and a unique nondegenerate alignment optimum, a
global input rotation is absorbed by the ﬁtted Q, so K(gX ) = K(X) in exact arithmetic. Since
the operation is rigid, kK(X)i   K(X)jk2 = kXi   Xjk2 for all pairs. Thus the canonicalization
operation itself does not ﬂatten loops or change heavy/light relative arrangement. This statement is
not an empirical guarantee for the encoder–quantizer–decoder composition.
The candidate contract requires centered-anchor singular-value ratio at least 10−3, cross-covariance
ratio at least 10−4 and joint framework-ﬁt RMSD at most 4 Å. These are proposed admission
rules, not established biological coverage guarantees. Symmetry, nearly degenerate ﬁts, number-
ing changes, quantization boundaries and sorting ties can still produce discontinuities. Each stored
frame is ﬁtted independently, so transform jitter across an ensemble must be reported. Repeated
structures and legacy bytes require separate compatibility tests.

9 D ISCUSSION AND LIMITATIONS
The evidence supports a speciﬁc and useful separation. Native-pose coordinate reconstruction can
be accurate with the declared exact sidecar, while a learned representation remains highly sensitive
to arbitrary global rotation. Production reboxing can worsen latent consistency and simultaneously
avoid the large decoded discrepancies of a raw rotated-box diagnostic. A method that is judged by
only one of these endpoints can therefore be mischaracterized in either direction.
The exact sidecar is legitimate when disclosed and counted. The learned decoder’s exclusion of
carrier coordinates prevents one simple reconstruction shortcut, but it does not establish that the
learned block is sufﬁcient for biological tasks. The route comparison shows metadata dependence;
it does not prove that metadata alone explains reconstruction. Likewise, the proposed joint frame-
work alignment preserves geometry before encoding by construction, but it cannot restore already
truncated residues and has not been shown to preserve CDRs after the complete learned and quan-
tized path.
The evidence limitations are substantive. Historical reconstruction receipts lack a complete local
raw-output replay and immutable environment closure. Pose metrics are available per structure,
but original stored-token mirrors are incomplete locally. The tested cohorts do not establish broad
homology-separated generalization; one rotation per structure is not uniform robustness over SO(3).
The historical CDR truncation percentages are not independently recomputed. The original check-
point, production adapter and source snapshot are distinct artifacts, and the source’s nominal 512-
bead interface does not override the accepted increment’s N  256 scope. Results on this antibody
branch cannot be transferred to IDP , LLPS or protein–ligand representations.
10 C ONCLUSION
We studied a frozen antibody tokenizer through separate tests of information ﬂow, stored-byte pose
behavior and residue completeness. The archived comparisons show strong exact-sidecar recon-
struction, substantial rotation sensitivity in learned tokens, and a large compound preprocessing
effect that is not explained by a change in the underlying rigid geometry. These ﬁndings motivate
a joint framework-anchored representation contract that preserves internal geometry analytically
while making CDR recovery, anti-collapse behavior and downstream utility explicit empirical re-
quirements. The present contribution is the diagnosis, architecture speciﬁcation and testable design.
A demonstrated pose-robust, CDR-preserving successor remains to be established.
