Rotation sensitivity after BF16 storage in coarse-grained molecular
encoding and a conditional pocket-frame correction
Abstract
Accurate reconstruction and translation stability do not establish orientation-stable feature
storage. We examine saved BF16-roundtrip measurements for ﬁxed-order, 512-bead, four-frame
Dry-MARTINI protein–ligand windows with frames sampled at 20-fs intervals. An original
256-case audit reported median nominal learned-lane cosines of 0.6382065 under rotation and
0.9999963 under translation, with decoded transformation errors of 0.3493213 and 0.0006608 Å,
respectively. Three trained successor pilots exceeded the strict rotation-cosine criterion of 0.9999,
but none satisﬁed all frozen reconstruction criteria. A later zero-parameter geometric codec
instead separates covariant carrier ﬁelds from local temporal-position and force components
deﬁned using a deterministic pocket-conditioned topology frame. Its saved 256-case paired
comparison satisﬁed all four criteria: position, velocity and force-log-norm nRMSE were 2.1996×
10−5, 0.0016553 and 0.0027322, and median nominal learned-lane rotation cosine rounded to
1.0. Nevertheless, decoded rotation error remained nonzero at 0.0006329 Å, and the changed-
payload fraction was 0.0006143 across the full 160-channel nominal lane, including 139 reserved-
zero channels. Source analysis distinguishes raw-frame encoding from quantized-frame decoding.
The evidence supports approximate stored orientation stability within this conditioned setting,
not exact byte invariance, universal molecular canonicalization or a successful newly trained
neural successor.
1 Introduction and contributions
Molecular encodings can reconstruct coordinates accurately while retaining substantial dependence
on laboratory orientation. Reconstruction asks whether a decoder recovers a target state; invari-
ance asks whether equivalent descriptions produce equivalent features. These properties are logi-
cally distinct, especially when a payload combines explicit Cartesian ﬁelds with learned features.
Translation removal alone does not resolve rotation sensitivity, and numerical storage introduces
another distinction between exact-arithmetic symmetry and measured behavior after quantization.
This case study asks whether reconstruction accuracy or translation stability establishes rotation-
stable storage. The motivating saved audit concerns a coarse-grained trajectory encoder–decoder
(codec) with two channel groups (lanes): a 32-channel carrier and a 160-channel nominal learned
lane. It tested bﬂoat16 (BF16) values packed as unsigned 16-bit payloads, rather than only ﬂoating-
point encoder outputs. Its strong translation cosine coexisted with a much lower rotation cosine.
The focused contributions are threefold. First, we separate the original audit from later paired
comparisons and preserve the reconstruction failures of three trained alternatives. Second, we
analyze a subsequent zero-parameter codec that stores local vector components alongside a covariant
frame and Cartesian carrier ﬁelds. Third, we distinguish its exact-arithmetic local-frame argument
from its approximate stored-precision results. The retained older corpus contains 10,000,007,168

2 Related work
Frame averaging is an established strategy for constructing invariant or equivariant functions by
combining evaluations over transformed inputs [ 1]. The present codec does not perform frame
averaging: it selects one conditioned frame and stores local components in that frame.
Dym and colleagues identify continuity concerns in frame-based methods and canonicalization [ 2].
This motivates examining degeneracies and frame-selection boundaries, but does not experimentally
validate this codec or prove that its particular conditioned construction necessarily fails. Our
analysis concerns a ﬁxed bead order, an externally supplied pocket coordinate and proper rotations;
it is not a general canonicalization theorem or an exhaustive novelty assessment.
3 Scientiﬁc methods and validation protocols
Evidence and population
This is a retrospective analysis of saved simulated-trajectory reports and captured source, with
no additional simulations. Original Torch inference was not rerun and checkpoint bytes were not
deserialized. The original audit selected one four-frame window from each of 256 system records,
with 512 beads and adjacent 20-fs states. Its source selects among 64 nonoverlapping four-state
windows in each saved 256-state trajectory array, whose metadata specify a 10,000-step horizon.
The later comparisons use a frozen held-out manifest of 256 systems with 256 distinct seeds. Four
adjacent frames are temporally related observations, not four independent trajectory replicates.
Three identical saved original-audit records are treated as repeated records of one measurement,
not independent experiments.
The original audit applied one deterministic Haar-distributed SO(3) rotation per structure and
translations with each component sampled within [−4, 4] nm. Positions, velocities, forces and the
canonicalization center rotate together; translation changes positions and center but not velocities
or forces. Later frame-conditioned evaluations also rotate the supplied pocket coordinate. All
four positions are expressed relative to one ﬁxed trajectory-frame-0 center, calculated as the bead-
position mean in the loading source, rather than recentering every frame. The codec uses a ﬁxed
scalar cell length of 160 Å for coordinate storage; no cell-orientation variable is represented.
Saved evaluations used PyTorch 2.10.0 with CUDA 12.8 on NVIDIA H100 hardware, CUDA
BF16 autocast and disabled TF32. The original audit additionally reports deterministic algorithms
enabled. Tokens are rounded to BF16, viewed as uint16 for storage and bit-cast back before feature
comparison or decoding. The later geometric codec disables autocast internally for its ﬂoat32
geometric operations. Repeated encodings of unchanged inputs produced identical payloads in the
saved controls; this establishes determinism under those conditions, not scientiﬁc validity.

Conditioned geometric construction
The later codec requires ﬁxed bead order, a ligand mask and an externally supplied deterministic
pocket coordinate. The captured preparation source obtains this coordinate from the system builder
using each system’s seed and stores it in seed-linked metadata; the evaluator retrieves the coordinate

Crucially, encoding projects vectors using the raw, unquantized frame. Decoding recovers the
ﬁrst two axes from the BF16 carrier and orthonormalizes them before reconstructing the third.
The encoder does not use this recovered frame for projection. Raw and recovered frames need not
coincide, so the algebra above does not establish exact decoded covariance after storage.

4 Experimental results
Original rotation sensitivity
The original saved audit reports median rotation cosine 0.6382065415382385 versus translation co-
sine 0.9999963045120239. Median decoded rotation and translation errors are 0.34932129085063934
and 0.000660822115605697 Å. Thus passing the translation criterion did not establish orientation-
stable features. Moreover, 0.3113251 of nominal learned-lane uint16 entries changed under transla-
tion despite its near-unity cosine: cosine similarity and payload equality answer diﬀerent questions.
Table 2 keeps this audit separate from the later paired baseline, whose rotation cosine is 0.6273563
and decoded rotation error 0.3495788 Å. These are distinct saved measurements, not evidence of
improvement or regression between baseline versions. The original raw tensors were not rerun here.

The geometric carrier’s median rotation cosine is 0.9501762. Its change is expected because it
stores Cartesian positions, velocities and frame axes. Requiring that raw carrier to be invariant
would contradict its covariant role.
5 Discussion and limitations
The central result is a separation of scientiﬁc requirements. Translation stability, reconstruction
accuracy, feature orientation stability and decoded covariance must each be tested under the actual
storage precision. The trained pilots demonstrate the converse limitation as well: near-unity feature
cosine does not guarantee acceptable position, velocity or force reconstruction.
The geometric correction provides a useful conditional example. It assigns directional informa-
tion explicitly to a carrier and uses local components for the nominal learned lane. However, it
is an ordered, pocket-conditioned construction rather than universal continuous molecular canon-
icalization. The maximum-projected-moment selection can change at selection boundaries; tie
margins, perturbation robustness and generalization beyond this population are not established.
The raw-frame/recovered-frame diﬀerence further explains why exact-arithmetic local invariance
and ﬁnite-precision decoded behavior require separate arguments.
The evidence comprises saved reports, static source and 184 independent local checks of arith-
metic, cross-report consistency, frozen inequalities, source/held-out/bundle identity and code struc-
ture. Those checks do not constitute 184 experiments and do not reproduce Torch inference. The
available summaries do not support conﬁdence intervals, independent external replication, force-
ﬁeld validity or long-time trajectory stability.
Finally, preserving the outer 512 × 192 shape does not preserve token semantics. Older and
geometric payloads cannot be mixed or assumed compatible with the same downstream model.
The unchanged outer payload size establishes neither a compression advantage nor downstream
predictive quality. The older corpus remains unreencoded, so the later held-out result cannot be
extrapolated to that entire corpus.
6 Conclusion
Saved BF16-roundtrip measurements show that translation stability does not establish orientation-
stable molecular features. Three trained alternatives improved rotation cosine beyond the pre-
scribed criterion but failed the combined reconstruction requirements. A later zero-parameter
pocket-frame codec satisﬁed the four requirements in a saved 256-case paired comparison, while
retaining nonzero decoded rotation error and a small nonzero changed-payload fraction. Its sup-
ported conclusion is approximate stored orientation stability for ﬁxed-order, pocket-conditioned,
512-bead, four-frame, 20-fs windows. Exact decoded covariance, broader applicability and down-
stream scientiﬁc utility remain outside that evidence.
