## Local LUCAN sources

Source: `public/research/LUCAN_Physical_Intelligence_Whitepaper_v3.pdf`.

- Sections 3-4: controlled cross-scale tests and independent numerical verification support the platform discussion. Numerical verification is not independent clinical or manufacturing validation.
- BioFSI test (PDF pages 6-7): reactor-to-cell membrane coupling uses a calibrated CHO-cell surrogate. The virtual-work tolerance was not passed. The collection does not claim universal coupling validation, or transfer this surrogate result to CAR-T potency or clinical performance.
- Section 5.2 / Table 2 (PDF pages 11-12): 5 L to 500 L mAb pilot; 6.63 g/L harvest titer, 78.2% viability, 94.5% TFF recovery. Values in the article match the source. The source also reports 1.2% HMW aggregates; this is not converted into a general product-quality guarantee.
- Section 5.3 (PDF page 11): accelerated computational formulation screening is distinct from full-duration stability confirmation, which remains explicitly pending.
- CAR-T, viral-vector manufacturing, general sequence-to-finished-product prediction and LNP therapeutic performance are not established by the cited pilot; the application pieces retain their prospective scope.
- The legacy `UNIPHY_Biomanufacturing_Whitepaper.pdf` remains background material, not independent evidence. The new scale-up document is the public-facing successor.

The partner batch/analytical records referred to in LUCAN v3 were not supplied. Matching statements to this whitepaper does not independently verify those records. Public wording therefore remains “Shodh-reported.”
