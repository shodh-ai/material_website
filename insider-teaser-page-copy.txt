SLIDE 2 - THE PROBLEM
---------------------
The Trillion-Dollar “Valley of Death”

AI has accelerated discovery. But physical production is still slow, manual, and expensive.

The Disconnect
Foundation models like Evo 2 for biology and GNoME for materials can propose promising molecules, materials, proteins, electrolytes, catalysts, and chemicals at unprecedented speed.

But moving that candidate into a bioreactor, chemical reactor, battery line, or factory still requires manual, multi-stage experimentation.

The Cost
This creates a Valley of Death between the quantum/micro scale and the factory floor.

Industry average to cross the gap:
- 2.5 years
- $1.5M+ per iteration

The Reality
A battery can work on a computer screen and fail on the factory line.

A biologic can work in a flask and collapse in a bioreactor.

A material can pass lab tests and fracture during industrial production.

Physics is currently siloed.


SLIDE 3 - THE SOLUTION
----------------------
The World Foundational Model

We are building a continuous physics AI model that learns the thread of physics connecting molecules, materials, processes, and factories.

It operates across four continuous layers:

01 - Discovery Layer
Integrates atomic, molecular, biological, and material discovery.

02 - Mesoscale Physics Engine
The core bridge. Simulates the lab-to-production gap where most failures emerge: mixing, shear, heat transfer, mass transfer, reaction kinetics, phase behavior, degradation, impurities, and instability.

03 - Control Layer
Translates physics predictions into process parameters, operating windows, control policies, and experiment plans.

04 - Digital Twin Layer
Creates dynamic, physics-enabled twins for reactors, production lines, and factory-scale systems.
